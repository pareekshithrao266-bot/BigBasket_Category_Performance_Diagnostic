**Below Target - Critical:**
- Snacks & Beverages: ₹10,895 vs. a ₹13,000 target — 16.2% short.

## Recommendations

1. **Prioritize catalog and marketing investment in Fruits & Vegetables.** It is the
   furthest below target of any category (-18.4%), and produce is typically a high-frequency
   purchase category — closing this gap likely has outsized impact on repeat visits, not
   just category revenue.

2. **Review supplier performance for Snacks & Beverages.** At -16.2%, it is the second most
   critical category. Since this category sits just past the Dairy & Eggs watch threshold,
   a supplier or assortment review here (pricing, availability, or catalog breadth) is
   worth prioritizing before it slips further.
